A message from your big sister, made especially for you.

Move 01 · From my heart

Dear my sweet little sister, ♡

With your KJSEA exams just around the corner, I wanted to make you something that reminds you of who you are — especially on a day when you might feel nervous or unsure.

I admire how hardworking you are, how you keep going, and how you stand firm in what you believe in. Your faith in God is beautiful, your heart is strong, and your sweetness makes life brighter. I love your creativity, your supportive nature, and the way you show up for the people you care about.

Sometimes you may not realise how special those things are, so let me remind you: you are more than a student preparing for an exam. You are a thoughtful, brave, creative girl with a future full of possibilities.

As you sit for each paper, take a breath, read carefully, and trust what you have learned. If a question feels difficult, don't panic — pause, think, and move to the next question if you need to. One hard question does not erase all the things you know.

And please remember, little queen: you never have to earn my love with marks. I will be cheering for you before the exams, during them, and after the last paper. I hope you make yourself proud, too.

With all my love, always — your big sister. ❤️

Move 02 · Little notes for big feelings

Open one whenever you need it. 💌

Tap each note. Imagine your big sister sitting beside you, reminding you of these things.

Move 03 · A little chess wisdom

Think like a chess player. ♟

You don't win a game by panicking over one move. You pause, look at the board, think about your options, and make the best move you can.

That's how I want you to approach your exams, too. Take your time. Look carefully at what each question is asking. If you get stuck, breathe and consider your next move. You don't have to solve everything at once — just focus on the move in front of you.

And remember: a queen is powerful, but even a queen needs a plan. Your plan is to stay calm, use what you know, and keep going. ♕

Move 04 · A prayer for you

May God go with you. 🙏🏽

Dear God,

Please watch over my little sister as she prepares for her KJSEA exams. Give her wisdom to understand the questions, a calm mind to remember what she has learned, courage when she feels nervous, and strength when she feels tired.

Protect her health, guide her steps, and help her do her very best. Remind her that she is never alone and that her worth is not defined by a result. May her hard work bear fruit, and may she walk into each paper with peace and confidence.

Amen. ❤️

Final move · Always your big sister

Whatever the result, you are my girl. 👑

I hope you get results that make you happy, and I pray that good opportunities open up for you. But before any result comes, I want you to know this: I am already proud of your effort, your faith, your courage, and the person you are becoming.

Thank you for being your sweet, creative, strong-hearted, supportive self. Never lose the goodness in you. Keep standing for what is right, keep dreaming, keep learning, and keep trusting God through every chapter.

When you finish your last paper, please take a moment to celebrate yourself. You made it through something important, and that deserves to be recognised. 🎉

All the very best in your KJSEA, my little queen. ♕
You've got this. I love you endlessly. ❤️

— Your big sister, who is always in your corner ♡
